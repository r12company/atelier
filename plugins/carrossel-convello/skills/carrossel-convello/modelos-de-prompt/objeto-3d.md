# Objeto 3D

Vai grande ATRÁS da pessoa na capa e no final (um de cada lado, virados para o meio), ou atravessando a emenda entre dois slides.
Nunca pequeno, boiando na frente, nem com brilho em volta.

Formato: 1:1. Fundo branco puro (Gemini ou ChatGPT) ou transparente (ChatGPT, pedindo "transparent background"). O motor tira o fundo nos dois casos.

## Objeto do assunto (copie e troque o que está entre < >)

```text
A single 3D object: <what it is>, made of glossy blue plastic (blue #0A6BF0), rounded and chubby, with thickness, volume, soft studio reflections and small white details. Seen from the front, slightly tilted. The object is centered and fills about 70% of the image, whole, with no edge cut off. Background: pure white (#FFFFFF), flat, no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow and no reflection on the background. No text, no letters, no watermark.
```

## Logo de outra marca em 3D (post sobre uma marca)

Anexe a logo oficial em PNG (a skill baixa do Simple Icons ou do site da marca e salva em `referencias/`). Sem anexo o gerador inventa o desenho (já saiu uma flor no lugar do ChatGPT).
No post de notícia mantenha as cores da marca; azul do Convello só se quem pediu mandar (foi o caso do WhatsApp azul do carrossel 02).

```text
A single 3D object: the logo from the attached reference image, exactly the same design, turned into a chunky glossy 3D badge with thickness and volume, in the brand's original colors. Seen from the front, slightly tilted and turned about 12 degrees, with soft studio reflections on top. The design stays correct and readable, not mirrored. The object is centered and fills about 70% of the image, whole, with no edge cut off. Background: pure white (#FFFFFF), flat, no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow. No extra text, no watermark.
```

## O mesmo objeto do outro lado (para não repetir o desenho quando aparecem dois)

Anexe o primeiro objeto aprovado:
```text
The same 3D object as in the attached reference image, with the same material, the same color and the same light, but seen from the other side: turned to the right, so the thickness shows on the RIGHT side and at the bottom, and slightly tilted back. The design stays correct and readable, not mirrored. Background: pure white (#FFFFFF), flat, no shadow. No text.
```

## Exemplo que deu certo (carrossel 02: o ícone do WhatsApp azul)

```text
A single 3D object: the WhatsApp icon in BLUE, same design as the attached reference image. A glossy blue rounded square (blue #0A6BF0) with very rounded corners, thickness and volume, like a chubby plastic button, with the chat bubble and the phone in WHITE in the center, embossed. Seen from the front, slightly tilted and turned about 12 degrees, with soft studio reflection on top. The icon is blue, never green. The object is centered and fills about 70% of the image, whole, with no edge cut off. Background: pure white (#FFFFFF), flat, no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow and no reflection on the background. No text, no letters, no watermark.
```

Refaça se tiver: placa ou quadrado atrás do objeto, cor errada, desenho da marca errado, texto.
